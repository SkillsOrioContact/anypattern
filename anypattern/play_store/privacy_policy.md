# Privacy Policy for AnyPattern

**Effective Date:** May 23, 2024

At AnyPattern, we take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines how we handle the information you provide when using our mobile application.

## 1. Local Data Storage
AnyPattern is designed as an offline-first application. All of your personal data, including measurement profiles, project drafts, and saved patterns, are stored locally on your device using an encrypted local database. We do not transmit, collect, or store this data on our servers.

## 2. Google Drive Backups
AnyPattern offers an optional feature allowing you to back up your local database to your personal Google Drive account.
- If you choose to use this feature, the app will request permission to access your Google Account (`drive.appdata` scope).
- The backup is uploaded exclusively to a hidden app-specific folder within your own Google Drive.
- We cannot access your Google Drive data, nor do we share your Google Account details with any third parties.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the free version of the app. AdMob may collect and use certain data (such as device identifiers and location data) to provide personalized ads. You can manage your ad preferences through your device settings.

## 4. AI Integration (Optional)
AnyPattern allows you to connect third-party AI services (e.g., Google Gemini) by providing your own API key.
- Your API key is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms.
- Your key is only transmitted directly to the respective AI provider (e.g., Google) to process your requests.
- We do not intercept, collect, or store your API keys on our servers.

## 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us.
